# v1.0-public-review

Initial AQDS public-review release.

Permanent record: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22746711

## Included

- the canonical paper in PDF and Markdown;
- three explanatory figures;
- source, claim and bounded prior-art ledgers;
- the registered SymPy derivation result;
- reproducibility scripts and pinned dependencies;
- release hashes and explicit proof boundaries.

## Verified Locally

- all nine David-approved main sections are preserved;
- 100 mathematical display blocks are present;
- 10/10 registered symbolic identities reduce to zero in SymPy 1.14.0;
- all registered Navier-Stokes momentum terms match acceleration dimensions;
- 100/100 equations and all three figures were included by both document
  builders;
- the 28-page canonical PDF passed automated bounds, page-content, metadata,
  heading, glyph and raster checks;
- all 28 canonical PDF page images were visually inspected;
- the generated DOCX passed structural and accessibility checks, but native
  DOCX visual pagination remains `Not verified yet` and the DOCX is therefore
  not part of the initial public package.

## Proof Boundary

This release is not peer reviewed. It does not independently verify the full
source proof or Lean artifact, establish a new Navier-Stokes proof, resolve the
unforced Clay Millennium problem, validate David Penny's physical-time
hypothesis, or establish novelty or consensus.
